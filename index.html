PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Tools for each stage of a response.

Start with a report, coordinate the response, and review what still needs attention.

🚨

Incident Command

Record an emergency, review its priority, assign a team and update its progress.

🛰️

Situational Map

Use the operations view to see the sample locations and response resources together.

🚑

Rescue Coordination

Review team skills and availability before choosing who should respond.

🏥

Medical Capacity

Keep key hospital capacity figures visible while coordinating medical support.

📦

Resource Allocation

Record relief supplies, quantities and allocations so shortages are easier to spot.

📢

Emergency Alerts

Prepare warning records and keep a history of the alerts entered in the system.
